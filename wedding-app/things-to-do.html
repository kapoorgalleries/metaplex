Things to Do

Coming early or staying on? A few of our favourite ways to enjoy Udaipur.

Landmark

City Palace

A sprawling lakeside palace complex with courtyards, museums and sweeping views over Lake Pichola.

On the water

Lake Pichola boat ride

Take a sunset boat to Jag Mandir island — the same water our ceremony overlooks.

Views

Sajjangarh (Monsoon Palace)

Perched on a hilltop for the best panorama of the city and surrounding Aravalli hills.

Wander

Old City & Bagore Ki Haveli

Narrow lanes, artisan shops, and a restored haveli with an evening folk-dance show.

Garden

Saheliyon Ki Bari

The "garden of the maidens" — fountains, lotus pools and marble pavilions. A calm morning stop.

Eat

Rooftop dinners

Udaipur's rooftop restaurants serve Rajasthani thali with a lake view. Ask the concierge for picks!
