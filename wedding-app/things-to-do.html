Things to Do

Coming early or staying on? A few of our favourite ways to enjoy New York.

The classic

Central Park

The green heart of Manhattan — perfect for an autumn stroll among the changing leaves.

Culture

World-class museums

The Met, MoMA and the Natural History Museum are all a short ride away — a rainy-day dream.

Views

Skyline views

Top of the Rock, the Empire State, or the Edge for the skyline; or the Staten Island Ferry (free!) past the Statue of Liberty.

Wander

The High Line & Chelsea

A garden walkway above the streets, ending near Chelsea Market and The Lighthouse at Pier 61.

Nearby

Battery Park & the harbor

Right by the Conrad — waterfront paths, the harbor, and ferries to the Statue of Liberty and Ellis Island.

Eat

Eat your way through NYC

A bagel and a slice, dumplings in Chinatown, or a Broadway show with dinner. Ask the concierge for picks!
