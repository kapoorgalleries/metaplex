FAQ

Still have a question? Tap the 💬 concierge or email us — we're happy to help.

What should I wear?

Indian festive attire is warmly encouraged across all events. The Haldi calls for something you don't mind getting a little yellow; the Sangeet is colourful and celebratory; the ceremony is traditional; the reception is Indian traditional or black tie. When in doubt, dress to celebrate.

Any colours to avoid?

Please refer to each event on the Schedule to see which colours/outfits are recommended.

Can I bring a guest?

Your invitation and RSVP reflect the seats reserved for you. If you have a question about your party, please reach out and we'll be happy to help.

What's the weather like?

Early November in New York is crisp — generally 40–55°F (5–13°C). Bring a wrap or coat for the evenings.

Getting between venues?

Everything is in Manhattan, a short ride apart. Taxis and rideshare are plentiful. Please note that shuttle service from the airport to the hotel will not be provided — we kindly ask guests to arrange their own transportation. Group transport between venues is separate, and those details will follow.

Where should I stay?

We've reserved a block of rooms at the Conrad New York Downtown, our home base for the weekend. Book from the Travel & Stay page using group code KMWED26, and reserve by October 6, 2026 to secure the group rate.

Will there be vegetarian food?

Our wedding meals will be served buffet-style, with many vegetarian options available. Guests with nut allergies should notify a banquet server before approaching the buffet so the team can provide guidance regarding the available dishes.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

Yes, we'd love that! During a few sacred moments we may gently ask for phones down so everyone can simply be present — our photographers will capture every part to share with you afterwards.

When should I RSVP, and who do I ask?

Kindly RSVP by August 31, 2026 on the RSVP page. For anything at all, email our planners at sonal@sjsevents.com (cc ginny@sjsevents.com) — they're handling every detail and will take wonderful care of you.
